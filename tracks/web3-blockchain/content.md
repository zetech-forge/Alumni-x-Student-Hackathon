# Web3 & Blockchain: Content & Curriculum

Everything here is optional prep. The Day 1 bootcamp covers the basics for anyone who arrives with no preparation. Links to the learning materials are in [Curated Resources](resources.md).

## Learning objectives
By the end, you should be able to:

- Explain in plain words what a blockchain, a wallet and a smart contract are
- Write, deploy and call a simple Solidity contract in Remix
- Write rules that must revert, and test that they do
- Connect a web page to a deployed contract
- Say why your solution needs a blockchain, and which UN SDG it serves

## Prerequisite knowledge
- Basic programming in any language (variables, functions, if statements)
- Nothing about blockchain. We start from zero.
- For Challenges B and C: comfort with a code editor and a terminal. Challenge C also needs some JavaScript.

## Topic breakdown

### Module 1: What is a blockchain?
A shared record that no single party controls, and why people use one. Ends with the question judges will ask: why a chain and not a database? About 1 hour.

### Module 2: Wallets and keys
What an account is, how a private key proves you own it, and why you never share it. Remix and the starter kit give you practice wallets that are already funded. About 45 minutes.

### Module 3: Transactions and gas
How a change gets onto the chain, who pays, and what gas is. About 45 minutes.

### Module 4: Smart contracts
A program stored on the chain that anyone can call and nobody can quietly edit. About 1 hour.

### Module 5: Solidity basics in Remix
Variables, functions, payable functions and events, written and run in the browser with nothing to install. This is all Challenge A needs. About 3 hours.

### Module 6: Testing and "must revert" rules
How to make a contract refuse an action, and how to try to break your own rules. Judges test your must-revert rules live, so this is the most valuable module. About 2 hours.

### Module 7: Connecting a web page to a contract
How a page reads a contract and sends transactions to it. Needed for Challenge B and a plus for A. About 3 hours.

### Module 8 (optional): AI agents with spending limits
For Challenge C only. How an agent loop calls a language model, why the agent must never hold the owner's key, and how a contract wallet limits what it can spend. About 3 hours.

## Suggested timeline
There are about four weeks until the event. Two to three hours a week is enough.

| Week | Modules | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 and 2 | Understand the ideas. Read only, no setup. |
| 2 | 3, 4 and 5 | Write and run your first contract in Remix. |
| 3 | 6 | Add rules that revert and test them. Install the tools for [Starter Kits](starter-kits.md) if you plan Challenge B or C. |
| 4 | 7, and 8 if you plan Challenge C | Connect a page to a contract. Pick a challenge idea and an SDG. |

If you do no prep, the Day 1 bootcamp covers Modules 1 to 6 and a first look at Module 7. Module 8 is not covered in depth, so Challenge C teams should prepare it beforehand.
