# Frequently Asked Questions

**Who can participate?**
Any currently enrolled Zetech University student (ICT, Engineering, or
other interested student developers) or Zetech ICT/Engineering alumni and
finalists. See [General Rules](general-rules.md) for full eligibility.

**Do I need to already have a team?**
No. Team formation happens on Day 1 morning if you arrive without one --
see the [Schedule](schedule.md).

**Can I participate without an alumni on my team?**
No -- every team needs at least one Alumni/Finalist Collaborator. This is
core to how the event works, not a minor rule. If you're a student without
an alumni connection, team formation on Day 1 is designed to help you find
one.

**What if I'm a beginner?**
Every track offers a Beginner-tier entry point. Check your track's
Challenges page -- most are structured so a working, modest solution
scores well without needing advanced experience.

**Can I use AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, etc.)?**
Yes, across every track, as long as you disclose what you used in your
submission. See [General Rules](general-rules.md).

**Can I use existing libraries, frameworks, and open datasets?**
Yes, unless your track's own Rules page says otherwise.

**Can I build with hardware?**
Hardware is central to the IoT & Robotics track -- see that track's
Resources page for what's provided. Other tracks are software-only unless
stated otherwise.

**What exactly do I submit?**
See "What every team submits" in [General Rules](general-rules.md) -- a
repo, README, demo video, pitch deck, and a few short written sections.

**How long is the build period?**
Two days, 12-13 November, with a submission freeze partway through Day 2 --
see the [Schedule](schedule.md). It's a two-day hackathon, not a 48-hour
one.

**Are there prizes?**
Yes -- see [Prizes](prizes.md) for the full structure.

**Who owns our project after the hackathon?**
You do. See "Intellectual property" in [General Rules](general-rules.md).
