# Prizes

*(Exact physical prizes and sponsors to confirm before publishing -- the
structure and categories below are ready.)*

## <i class="fa-solid fa-trophy ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> Grand Prize
**Overall Hackathon Winner** -- selected during the Grand Finale from the
**15 finalists** (top three from each of five tracks), using the
[two-stage framework](general-rules.md#awards--judging).
Each finalist carries a technical score out of 100 and completes the common
[From Prototype to Real-World Impact](real-world-impact.md) challenge for
30 additional points: maximum 130, normalized as `(Combined Score / 130) * 100`.
Trophy, a mechanical keyboard per team member, certificates, a feature in
university media, and priority introductions to partner organizations.

## Track Champions
One per track: the highest-ranked team by its **average independent judge
score out of 100** in the Track-Level technical assessment.
The top **three**, not only the champion, qualify for the Grand Finale.
- <i class="fa-solid fa-laptop-code ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> Best Software Development Solution
- <i class="fa-solid fa-robot ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> Best AI, Data Science and Machine Learning Solution
- <i class="fa-solid fa-lock ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> Best Cybersecurity Solution
- <i class="fa-solid fa-link ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> Best Web3 and Blockchain Solution
- <i class="fa-solid fa-gears ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> Best IoT and Robotics Solution

Medal, plaque, branded hoodie/T-shirt, and a laptop sleeve.

## Grand Finale Special Awards
- <i class="fa-solid fa-earth-africa ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> **Best Social Impact Solution**
- <i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> **Most Innovative Solution**
- <i class="fa-solid fa-person-chalkboard ztf-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i> **Best Pitch & Presentation**

These awards are determined during Grand Finale Pitch & Awards Judging.
The Alumni Contribution submission remains required, but is not a separate
award category in this structure. Physical prizes and the special-award
selection procedure require organizer confirmation.

Plaque, a university tote bag, and a small tech accessory.

## Everyone who competes
Certificate of participation, lanyard, and stickers.

## What happens after the hackathon

Winning teams don't just walk away with a trophy. Zetech Forge continues
as a **Post-Hackathon Innovation Pathway**:
- Mentorship from the alumni who judged or coached your track
- Incubation referral and iZET Hub support
- Industry introductions through partner organizations
- A spot in the project showcase
- The opportunity to keep developing your prototype after the event
