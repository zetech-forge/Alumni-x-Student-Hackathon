# General Rules & Regulations

*Applies to every track. Track-specific rules live on each track's own "Track Rules & How to Compete" page.*

## Event theme
**Connecting Minds, Shaping Sustainable Futures: Alumni and Students in Collaborative Innovation and Research**

## When & where
- **Dates:** 12-13 November 2026, 9:00 AM start each day
- **Venue:** Zetech University, Ruiru Campus Auditorium, plus two breakout rooms
- **Capacity:** ~150 participants

## Eligibility
- **Students:** any currently enrolled Zetech University student, any year, pursuing ICT or Engineering, plus other interested student developers.
- **Alumni:** Zetech ICT and Engineering alumni, and final-year ("finalist") students.
- No separate age restriction beyond current enrollment or alumni status.

## Team composition
- Teams must have **3-4 members**.
- Each team must include **at least one Alumni/Finalist Collaborator**, responsible for contributing at least one of: domain knowledge, problem validation, industry perspective, product feedback, technical mentorship, pitch feedback, or industry feasibility assessment. This is a working role, not just an eligibility checkbox -- see the submission requirements below.
- Each team selects **one team lead** from among its own members.
- A participant may only be on one team.

## Choosing a track
- Each team competes in **one** track for the full event: Software Development, Web3 & Blockchain, IoT & Robotics, Machine Learning & Data Science, or Cybersecurity.
- **Every team must align its solution with at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG)**, and must answer three questions in their submission, not just name the SDG:
  - **Problem** -- what real problem are you solving?
  - **Beneficiary** -- who experiences this problem?
  - **Impact** -- what measurable change could your solution create?

  "Our app relates to SDG 9" is not enough on its own. "Our solution reduces stock-out detection delays for rural health facilities by giving clinic staff an offline inventory and sync workflow" is the bar.
- Track-specific rules, judging criteria, and challenges are under each track's own section of this site.

## Registration
Registration opens via [REGISTRATION LINK] and closes [DATE]. See the [Register](register.md) page for the current status and link.

If a team is short a member or loses one before the event, they may add a replacement up to 48 hours before the event starts, provided the team still meets the 3-4 member and Alumni/Finalist Collaborator requirements. After that point, incomplete teams compete as-is or are merged with organizer approval.

## What every team submits
Regardless of track, every team's final submission must include:
1. A GitHub repository with their code
2. A README with setup/run instructions
3. A 2-3 minute demo video (required as a backup even if you also demo live -- live demos can fail on flaky wifi or APIs)
4. A short pitch deck
5. A live demo URL, if applicable
6. Team member details, including who served as the Alumni/Finalist Collaborator
7. An **Alumni Contribution** note: what did your alumni/finalist member actually contribute? (see Team composition above)
8. An SDG impact statement (see SDG alignment below)
9. The technology stack used
10. An AI-tool disclosure (see below)
11. Known limitations

## AI usage
AI-assisted development is permitted across every track. Teams must disclose significant use of AI coding assistants, generative AI systems, pretrained models, and APIs in their submission -- which tools, and roughly how much they contributed. This is for transparency; it is not penalized unless undisclosed.

## Intellectual property
Participants retain ownership of their original intellectual property unless a separate written agreement states otherwise. If your solution depends on a sponsor-provided API, open-source license, or dataset, note that dependency and its license in your README. By participating, teams agree that Zetech University may photograph, film, and showcase their project (including after the event) for university and partner communications.

## How the two days flow

**Day 1 — Learn & Build**
Opening session → Keynote → Technology bootcamps (per-track crash courses) → Team formation → Challenge briefing → Ideation → Prototype development

**Day 2 — Build & Pitch**
Continued development → Mentor consultations → Prototype testing & pitch prep → Demonstrations → Judging → Awards → Networking → Closing (with a Chief Guest closing the event)
